[Knowember](https://robertbirming.com/knowember/) is a November blogging event (!): 

everyone answers the same few questions on their own blog, so we get to know each other and discover new blogs along the way. Thanks to [Robert Birming](https://robertbirming.com/) for putting it together, it's exactly my kind of thing!

Funny story, though: I found out about it on **Bluesky**. Which wouldn't be strange at all, if it weren't for the fact that Robert himself [lives on a Mastodon server](https://social.lol/@birming), and I've been hanging around the fediverse for well over four years now without ever running into him!

That's a perfect little example of something that has bugged me for a while:

**The fediverse is supposed to be _the_ place for people who still believe in personal blogs and the small web, and yet finding them there is a side quest with no map**: 

no algorithm (which is good, mostly), a search that only recently stopped being useless, and a timeline that, at least in my corner, looks a lot like a "progressive Twitter", where news and politics crush every niche topic like video games, music or, well, blogging events. Federation is great on paper, but in practice each server sees its own slice of the network, and if nobody on yours happens to boost the right post, that post simply doesn't exist for you.

I've written about it [more](/posts/fediverse-language/) than [once](/posts/mastodon-bluesky-english/), so I won't start ranting again (I promise, this is the short version!), but it's a bit ironic that a nice, decentralised, community-driven thing like this reached me through the one network that's _not_ supposed to be the cosy one.

## Name or nickname

Like the URL says, I'm Andrea Corinti (ciao!), but online pretty much everyone knows me as "Xab".

It comes from **Xabaras**, the villain of [Dylan Dog](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dylan_Dog), the Italian horror comic by Tiziano Sclavi: a mad scientist who raises the dead chasing immortality (and, spoiler, Dylan's own father). His name is an anagram of [Abraxas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abraxas), a Gnostic "Great Archon" whose Greek letters add up to 365, later demoted to a demon and sometimes linked to _abracadabra_.

The full handle, _Xabacadabra_, comes from _Sabbra Cadabra_ by Black Sabbath: back on MSN, when your nickname showed the song you were listening to, the two just sounded too good together!

And if you're wondering what it says on my About page, my bio has been the same for ages: _"Bio is a Poison-elemental attack that can inflict the Poison status."_ Yes, it's a Final Fantasy spell, and no, I'm not changing it!

## Where in the world I blog from

Good question, because the answer kept changing! I was born in 1989 in the hills around Como, in northern Italy, then spent my teenage years bouncing between Argentina and Sicily. After that came about ten years in Genoa and six in Athens (2019–2025), and now I'm back in Lombardy.

My very first blogging experiments actually started in Argentina, around 2003–2004: I was a teenager far from home, eager to keep in touch with Italy and with a whole nerd universe I was discovering for the first time, thanks to the internet.

With all that wandering, I like to call myself an "Italian Web World Citizen", which sounds a bit grand, but the internet has honestly been the only place I never moved away from!

## What my blog is mostly about

A bit of everything I care about, but if I look back at the 300-something posts here, a few things keep coming back:

- **Final Fantasy**, above everything else. It's not just a series I love, it pretty much shaped my whole life online: in 2003, in Argentina, I was spending my days in internet points downloading the trailers of _Advent Children_ (the Final Fantasy VII film) and burning them on DVDs, and I ended up as a moderator of _Your Final Fantasy Community_, my very first "job" on the web. Around twenty posts here have Final Fantasy in the title, from the Pixel Remasters to my long (and not exactly kind) thoughts on the VII Remake;
- **video games** in general, mostly JRPGs and old stuff, which these days I play on the Switch 2 more than anything else;
- **music**, metal above all, with my monthly _Disco del Mese_ ("record of the month") series going on since 2024;
- **films, TV series and anime**;
- **the internet** itself: the fediverse, the indie web (for a while I took part in the _Sagra IndieWeb_, a monthly themed blogging event among Italian bloggers in the fediverse, a bit like Knowember, though I've kind of dropped it lately), RSS, and how much I miss the old forums.

The blog has been around for more than ten years and it's written mostly in Italian, but everything I write in English ends up in the [English filter](/categoria/english-0/). The spirit is the same as on the home page: let's pretend it's still 2003, when a personal blog was just a place to ramble about your stuff and see who shows up!

And people do show up, sometimes: under the posts you'll find the replies from Bluesky and the fediverse, so if you want to say hi, that's the place!
