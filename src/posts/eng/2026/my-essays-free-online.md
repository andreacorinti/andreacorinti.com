Last night I didn't sleep, and the site took advantage of it:

a post about Linux, some SEO nerdery I'll spare you, an [About page](/en/about/) with a Final Fantasy touch and above all the biggest thing, which I'll get to in a moment.

To do it I used Claude, Anthropic's AI, a lot, and I mean a lot:

with all the problems AI carries around (and there are many, eh), certain optimizations that would take me weeks if not months (or years of procrastination...) get solved in one sleepless night.

## "Aren't you worried it copies your data and has access to your stuff?"

Honestly no, or better, it's not like I could do much about it:

I'm not an artist, I live of written words and flying ideas, sharing passions and helping to manage certain things, so my point of view is very different from the one of someone who draws and sees a machine copying their style without permission.

Sure, there would be the whole talk about web design or programming in general, but I won't tell you how many more or less messy copy and pastes I did from Stack Overflow, GitHub and so on...

ok, I was wandering off, but that's also the point: this blog is public, what I write on social media is public (even with some privacy settings I honestly don't believe much in) and the licenses are there, but my "little" contribution to LLM training was given a long time ago, since everything here has been on GitHub for years, where Copilot rules but above all it's all in an open repository anyone can see (and don't make me think about the Blogspot days).

## Goodbye Amazon, goodbye itch.io

The big thing is this: today I definitively cut ties with Amazon self-publishing and with distribution via itch.io.

They're not intrinsically evil services, let's be clear (well, Amazon a bit yes 😄), but basically they're not for me.

With the paperback of *Giochi Troppo!* (the Italian edition of *Too Much Gaming!*), back in 2022, I made a little money, enough to cover the cover art by Capitan Troll (Alessandro Rinnovati) and the Tifa illustration by Martina Masaya, plus some other little things here and there, but it was more the self-centered satisfaction of seeing myself printed on paper without losing money.

But here's the thing: selfishness, pollution, support to an ugly multinational... thinking about it again I believe it was a practical choice, and I'm happy with my essay as a testimony of the post-Covid period, even if I realize very well I'm certainly not worth the printing costs (it's not false modesty, between you and me I think it about almost everything that gets written and printed nowadays, and maybe that's also why I threw myself into something like this).

## Where to read them

Long story short, from today my essays are free to read right here, chapter by chapter, and you can download the EPUBs without wandering around itch.io (where they were already free anyway):

- [Too Social](/books/too-social/), and in Italian [Troppo Social](/libri/troppo-social/)
- [Too Much Gaming!](/books/too-much-gaming/)

The Italian *Giochi Troppo!* has to wait until February: there's still an Amazon exclusivity that ends in late January, then it will join the others.

At the end of every chapter there's also a Liberapay link, but what matters is that you read them 😌
