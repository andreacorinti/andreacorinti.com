The second part of this book is dedicated to three conversations about video games with the following people:

Fausto, a programmer and philosopher living abroad in Scotland, passionate about gaming since the arcade days;

Annalisa, a musician and teacher, fiercely in love with JRPGs, graphic adventures, and rhythm games;

Manuela, a psychologist and circus performer with a gaming passion that's practically a family trait, plus a hobby for cosplay;

Even though they come from completely different backgrounds, as you've probably gathered, all three share a deep passion for the gaming world they essentially grew up alongside: drawing on their respective areas of expertise and life paths, I tried to take stock of where gaming stands today and imagine what's waiting for us in the near future.

Even though they started out as analytical interviews, feel free to think of them as simple conversations between friends that, starting from video games, end up touching on a lot of different topics.
