Andrea Corinti, aka Xab, is a self-proclaimed “Italian Web World Citizen”.

Born in 1989 in the hills around Como, he grew up between Argentina and Sicily, then spent about ten years in Genoa and six in Athens, before finally coming home.

He’s changed addresses quite a few times, but his inventory never did: the internet, music, books, video games, movies and comics (plus a suspicious stack of Potions).

Today he works as a freelancer in e-learning content and support, and writes on his blog.
