*Too Much Gaming!* is an invitation to think about how much of our lives we spend on video games and, at the same time, a look at how important that world has become for all of us.

Through quotes and artwork, it travels across gaming from Monkey Island all the way to the galaxy of streamers, closing with three interviews with players from unusual backgrounds: a musician-teacher, a philosopher-programmer and a psychologist.
