> *"Have you ever thought about it? When you were children... The feelings back then... The words back then... The emotions back then... And when you grow up... what's left of it? What do you throw away?*
>
> *Time doesn't wait. No matter how tightly you hold onto it. Time slips away. And..."*
>
> *(Ultimecia, Final Fantasy VIII)*

As I wrote at the start of this little book, I'll never get tired of declaring my love for video games:

I consider them a fundamental part of who I became as a person, and, why not, of who I am.

I haven't hidden it, but this book is often pretty self-referential, and I hope you'll forgive me for that:

if it is, it's because it's mainly meant to speak to my own *tribe* — the people who, for purely generational and social reasons, went through gaming adventures similar to mine.

But beyond that, there's also a half-hearted ambition to help people a bit older than that understand certain dynamics, people who'd like to step into the world of their kids or grandkids to understand why they spend 50% of their day on *Fortnite* and its relatives.

As for younger readers, I hope I can at least say one thing: be careful.

In general, all of us should think a bit harder about how we distribute the most precious thing we have:

**time.**

To quote José Mujica,[^57] when we buy something, we're not really buying it with money, we're buying it with the life-time we spent earning that money.

Our life's time is something nothing and no one can ever give back to us, and yet, despite everything, we tend to squander it.

And to be clear: spending time gaming can be a great investment, because, in some way, video games enrich us, almost always giving us something back.

The trick, like in relationships, is knowing when we're giving a lot while getting far too little back in return.

*"If only one could avoid dying!*

*If only life could be brought back, what an eternity that would be!*

*And it would all be mine!*

*Then I would turn every minute into a whole century,*

*I wouldn't waste a single one, I would keep exact count of every minute,*

*I would no longer squander anything in vain!"*

Fyodor Dostoevsky
