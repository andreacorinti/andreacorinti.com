First of all, I really hope you enjoyed this little book and the "imaginary dialogue" it's structured around, which, I realize, is a choice that might rub some people the wrong way.

The truth is I hadn't planned on writing it this way, but as I worked through the drafts that make up this text, much like what often happens with posts on my blog, I found it more fun and natural to handle it this way.

I'm well aware that I left out several points, and wasn't exhaustive on some of the ones I did cover, but my goal was to make the topic as light as possible, and to get you curious enough to dig deeper by reading far more important texts, drawing on the bibliography that follows.

In particular, I'll admit that putting it together and finding texts that met my needs was more complicated than expected — much more so than with *Too Much Gaming*.
