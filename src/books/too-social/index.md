A short essay about our troubled relationship with social media: how much time do they steal from us, how much do we depend on them?

It follows the line of [Too Much Gaming!](/books/too-much-gaming/) and its take on gaming disorder, but this time on a far more universal subject: social networks, our dependence on them, and the alternative offered by the Fediverse.
